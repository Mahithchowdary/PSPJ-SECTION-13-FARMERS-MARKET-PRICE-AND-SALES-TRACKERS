FARMER’S   MARKET  PRICE &  SALES TRACKERS 

ABSTRACT – Farmers Markets Price & Sales Trackers                           
The Farmers' Market Price & Sales Tracker is a simple and useful software project developed to help farmers and market sellers manage their products, prices, and daily sales in an organized way. Farmers usually deal with different types of products such as vegetables, fruits, grains, and other agricultural items. Keeping track of the price, quantity sold, and total income manually can take more time and may lead to calculation mistakes. This project provides an easy solution for maintaining these details digitally.
The system allows the user to enter the name of the agricultural product, price per unit, and quantity sold. Based on the entered information, the program automatically calculates the sales amount using the formula Total Sales = Price × Quantity. It can also calculate the total sales for multiple products using a loop. This makes the process faster and reduces the need for repeated manual calculations.
The project is implemented using Java programming concepts such as variables, data types, user input, arithmetic operators, loops, methods, and output statements. A separate method is used for calculating the sales amount, making the program easier to understand and maintain. The system displays the sales amount for each product and finally shows the total sales amount.
The main purpose of this project is to provide a simple, user-friendly, and efficient way to track market sales. It can help small-scale farmers and sellers maintain basic sales records and understand their daily income more easily. The project also demonstrates how programming can be used to solve a practical real-life problem through a simple computerized system.

ADVANTAGES OF FARMERS MARKETS PRICES & SALES TRACKERS
1.	Easy to Maintain Records – Farmers can easily record product names, prices, and quantities sold.
2.	Saves Time – The system quickly calculates sales amounts instead of doing calculations manually.
3.	Reduces Calculation Errors – Automatic calculations reduce mistakes in finding the total sales.
4.	Tracks Multiple Products – Different products such as vegetables, fruits, grains, and other farm products can be recorded.
5.	Easy Price Tracking – Farmers can enter and keep track of the selling price of each product.
6.	Calculates Total Sales – The system automatically calculates the total amount earned from the products sold.
7.	Simple to Use – The program has a simple input and output system, making it easy for users to understand.
8.	Improves Organization – Product and sales information can be maintained in a structured manner.
9.	Useful for Daily Sales – Farmers can use the tracker to calculate their sales for each market day.
10.	Supports Better Record Keeping – Maintaining sales information can help farmers understand their daily business activities and manage their products more effectively.
